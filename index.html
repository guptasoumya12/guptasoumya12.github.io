About

Soumya Gupta is an Applied AI Technical Lead with over ten years of experience building and scaling production-grade AI systems. At Uber, she leads computer vision and generative AI work for Uber Eats, owning end-to-end systems — from model development to large-scale infrastructure, latency optimization, and observability — that process millions of images globally.

Her recent work focuses on closed-loop evaluation for multimodal AI agents: a production pipeline that uses generative models to enhance food photography for independent merchants while preserving authenticity and guarding against reward hacking. She has presented this work at the AI Engineer World’s Fair, AI DevCraft, and NeurIPS.

Before Uber, Soumya was a Machine Learning Engineer at Schlumberger, where she built computer-vision and sensor-validation systems for oilfield drilling operations — work that resulted in three granted US patents, several pending applications, and peer-reviewed publications at SPE/IADC conferences. She holds a Master of Engineering from UC Berkeley and a B.E. (Honors) from BITS Pilani.
